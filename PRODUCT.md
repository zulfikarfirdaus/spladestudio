# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Businesses globally (SMB to mid-market) evaluating a web design and development partner. They're comparing studios, not casually browsing — they have a budget, a timeline, and a decision to make. Primary task on any screen: assess quality, feel confidence in the team, and take a concrete next step.

## Product Purpose

Splad Studio is a web design and development studio. This site is a conversion tool — prospective clients use it to vet the studio's craft and reach out. Success means the visitor leaves either with a formed opinion (strong enough to book) or an inquiry in motion.

## Brand Personality

Confident, direct, craft-obsessed. Three words: conviction, precision, momentum.

## Anti-references

- Generic Awwwards-clone agencies: rotating 3D blobs, gradient mesh heroes, portfolios that look identical to each other.
- Big-4 consulting style: corporate navy-blue enterprise aesthetic, Accenture/McKinsey visual language.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — Let the work speak. Copy supports the work, it doesn't compensate for it.
2. **Conviction, not hedging** — Every sentence earns its place. No qualifiers, no filler, no "we strive to."
3. **Speed is part of the product** — The 2-week delivery promise must feel visceral in the experience itself.
4. **Quality is consistent, not spotlit** — No section should be visually richer than another. The whole surface is the pitch.
5. **Familiar tools, unfamiliar execution** — Use established web conventions but execute them at a level that makes the visitor notice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best effort. No hard compliance target. Semantic HTML, readable contrast, keyboard navigation as defaults.
